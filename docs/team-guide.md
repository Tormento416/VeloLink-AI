## 1. What VeloLinkAI sells

VeloLinkAI helps local small businesses with three things:

1. **Websites** built from reusable frameworks (1-page, 5-page, 10-page), adapted with the client's approved content and checked by a person before launch.
2. **A managed AI phone receptionist** that answers calls 24/7, takes the caller's details, and books appointments.
3. **Local SEO marketing**, sold as a setup fee plus a monthly or annual plan.

Who we sell to: emergency trades (plumbers, electricians, HVAC, locksmiths), professional services (legal, accounting, consulting), and community contractors (landscaping, remodeling, roofing). We do not sell to medical or dental practices.

### 4.2 Commission rate on one-time fees

The rate is set by how many deals the representative completes in a calendar month. It resets on the 1st. The rate reached applies to every deal completed that month.

| Deals completed in the month | Commission rate |
|---|---|
| 1 to 2 | 25% |
| 3 to 4 | 30% |
| 5 to 6 | 35% |
| 7 or more | 40% |

A deal is **completed** when the client has signed the VeloLinkAI agreement and the first payment has been collected [PROPOSED]. It counts in the month the payment is collected.

### 4.3 When Adrian helps close [PROPOSED]

- The representative found the lead and ran the demo, Adrian helped on a call: full commission to the representative.
- Adrian closed it alone from a representative's lead: commission is paid at half the representative's rate for that month. [OPEN] Adrian to confirm.

### 4.4 Residual on retainers

- 5% of **net collected retainer** for each client the representative sold, for that client's first 12 months.
- **Net collected** means the retainer payments actually received, minus refunds, chargebacks and payment-processing fees [PROPOSED definition, Adrian to confirm].
- If the client renews after 12 months, the 5% continues for the renewal period [DECIDED]. What counts as a renewal on a month-to-month plan is [OPEN]. Suggested rule: the client is still active and paying in month 13.
- Whether the residual continues after a representative leaves is [OPEN].
- SEO pays no residual. Only the $799 SEO setup fee pays commission, at the one-time rate in section 4.2 [DECIDED].

### 4.5 How the draw works

The draw is an advance on commission, not a salary.

1. The draw starts after the representative's first completed sale [DECIDED]. From then on the representative receives $2,000 each month.
2. Commission and residuals earned that month are counted against the $2,000.
3. If earnings are more than $2,000, the representative is paid the extra.
4. If earnings are less than $2,000, the shortfall is carried forward as a draw balance and is paid down from future months' extra earnings.
5. If the representative leaves, any remaining draw balance is forgiven.

**Worked example**

| | Month 1 | Month 2 |
|---|---|---|
| Deals completed | 2 | 5 |
| One-time fees collected | $3,000 | $10,000 |
| Commission rate | 25% | 35% |
| Commission earned | $750 | $3,500 |
| Draw paid | $2,000 | $2,000 |
| Earned above the draw | $0 | $1,500 |
| Draw balance carried in | $0 | $1,250 |
| Applied to the balance | | $1,250 |
| Extra paid on top of the draw | $0 | $250 |
| **Total paid to the representative** | **$2,000** | **$2,250** |
| Draw balance carried out | $1,250 | $0 |

Residuals are left out of the example to keep it simple. They count as earnings the same way.

Still to decide [OPEN]:

- Whether the draw runs indefinitely or is reviewed after a set period.
- Whether there is a cap on the carried balance.

**[COUNSEL] A fixed monthly payment to a contractor can make the relationship look more like employment. Have the draw terms reviewed before the first draw is paid.**

### 4.8 Taxes and paperwork while on 1099

- Each representative gives VeloLinkAI a completed IRS Form W-9 before the first payment.
- No taxes are withheld. Representatives are responsible for their own income and self-employment taxes.
- VeloLinkAI issues a Form 1099-NEC after year end where the IRS threshold is met. [COUNSEL] Confirm the current threshold with the CPA.
- A signed Independent Sales Representative Agreement is required before the first draw or commission is paid. [OPEN] Not yet drafted.

### 4.9 Option to convert to W-2 employee after 90 days

After 90 days as a 1099 representative, the representative has the option to convert to a W-2 employee of VeloLinkAI [DECIDED]. The choice is the representative's. Staying on 1099 is also allowed.

What changes on conversion:

- The representative goes on payroll. Income tax, Social Security and Medicare are withheld from each paycheck, and VeloLinkAI pays the employer share.
- The representative completes a Form W-4 and Form I-9, and receives a W-2 at year end instead of a 1099-NEC.
- As an employee, the company can set schedules, call targets, required scripts and assigned duties such as client check-ins.
- An employee who sells by phone from home must be paid at least minimum wage for hours worked, and overtime where it applies, so hours have to be tracked [COUNSEL].

Still to decide [OPEN]:

- When the 90 days start (suggested: the date of the signed agreement) and how the representative gives notice (suggested: in writing, effective the 1st of the next month).
- What happens to any draw balance at conversion (suggested: it carries over).
- Whether a representative can later switch back to 1099.

Needed before the first conversion:

- A payroll provider and state employer registrations.
- An employee handbook and offer letter.
- Workers' compensation review. North Carolina requires cover at three or more employees [COUNSEL].
- [COUNSEL] Review of the conversion option itself. Offering the same job as either 1099 or W-2 can be used as evidence that the role was employment from the start.

## 6. Rules every representative follows

These are required, because they protect clients and the company.

1. **Money and logins.** Never take payment from a client and never hold a client's logins. VeloLinkAI bills and collects.
2. **Prices.** Quote only the prices marked [DECIDED] in section 3. The only standing discount is the veteran-owned business discount in section 3.5. Anything else needs Adrian's approval.
3. **What the AI receptionist does.** Describe each plan exactly as section 3.2 lists it, and do not promise a Basic client something only Premium does. It does not diagnose problems or give repair, legal, medical, electrical or gas-safety advice. [PROPOSED scope, Adrian to confirm]
4. **No guarantees.** Do not promise results such as "never miss a call", a search ranking, or a number of new customers.
5. **No income claims.** When talking to anyone who may join as a representative, do not state or suggest what they will earn.
6. **Honest introductions.** Do not use made-up stories about yourself or other clients.
7. **Calling rules.** Follow do-not-call and telemarketing rules. [COUNSEL] The specific rules for representatives have not been written yet.
8. **Confidentiality.** Prices, templates, prompts, client lists and this guide belong to VeloLinkAI and are not shared outside the team.
9. **Client relationships.** Clients are VeloLinkAI's clients. The contract, billing and accounts stay with VeloLinkAI.
